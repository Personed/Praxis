DOOM integration provenance
===========================

Engine: GZDoom g4.11.3 compiled to WebAssembly by the tomb-engine browser port,
upstream commit 6c735315b8ac1b1dd6646ac78c46bbbdbb775a5c.

- Port sources: https://github.com/mungus43/tomb-engine/tree/6c735315b8ac1b1dd6646ac78c46bbbdbb775a5c
- GZDoom sources: https://github.com/ZDoom/gzdoom/tree/g4.11.3
- License: GPL-3.0 (full text in doom-gzdoom-GPL-3.0.txt and in the shipped
  app assets at doom/gzdoom/UPSTREAM-LICENSE)
- Build recipe: doom/gzdoom/UPSTREAM-build.ps1
- Local modifications against the upstream binaries: doom/gzdoom/LOCAL-CHANGES.md
- Patch inventory: doom/gzdoom/UPSTREAM-PATCH_INVENTORY.md

Doom game data
--------------
The bundled DOOM1.WAD is the original shareware v1.9 release. Copyright id
Software / ZeniMax Media Inc. It is game data, not covered by the GPL that
applies to the engine source. The original distribution notices ship unmodified
at doom/game/README.TXT and the untouched archive at doom/game/doom19s-original.zip.
Registered DOOM/DOOM2 data, Plutonia, TNT and FreeDM are not bundled.
