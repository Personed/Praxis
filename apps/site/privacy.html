Privacy Policy

Controller

[RAZÃO SOCIAL], registered under [CPF/CNPJ], is the controller of personal data processed by Modus under Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018, LGPD). Contact: [E-MAIL DE CONTATO].

Data we process

Modus does not require a Modus account or sell credits. Conversations and settings are stored in your local profile. Requests to an external model provider use the credentials you configure for that provider.

Conversation content

Chats, files and projects stay on your computer. When you use a cloud model, the request is sent to the provider endpoint you configure to generate the response. Local models run on your computer and do not send this content.

Purposes and legal bases

Data is used to run the features you choose and to connect to the providers you configure.

Sharing

External model providers and connected services receive data needed for the operations you request. GitHub distributes application updates. Consult each provider’s privacy terms.

Retention

Local data remains in your profile until you remove it. External providers apply their own retention policies.

Your rights

You can request confirmation, access, correction, portability, anonymization or deletion of your data, or withdraw consent, at [E-MAIL DE CONTATO]. You may also complain to the ANPD.
